# Experimental E. coli growth datasets for frozen FluxTransformer transfer

## Scope and search

**Search date: 7 October 2026. Five independent study cohorts verified.** Per the user's clarification, parental laboratory strains qualify without further study modifications; knockout, reporter, selected-mutant and evolved derivatives do not. BW25113's existing arabinose/rhamnose/lacZ deletions do not directly affect Gerosa's selected substrates.

The requested notes/notebooks and Research Base were consulted. Train a front MLP using composition and measured growth through a frozen iML1515 reservoir; expanding simulation inputs and retraining the reservoir is allowed.

Searched PubMed/Europe PMC, publisher supplements, general scholarly web search, Figshare, Zenodo, Dryad, author GitHub repositories, ETH's repository, MediaDB, EcoCyc, PRECISE/iModulonDB and CeCaFDB, including references and later releases. Representative queries combined “E. coli defined media growth curves”, “mixed carbon specific growth rate”, “uptake secretion chemostat”, and MG1655/BW25113/NCM3722. EcoCyc/CeCaFDB timed out; Cell, ETH and PNAS downloads encountered blocking/403 responses; PMC/eLife pages sometimes required browser checks. Europe PMC XML and publisher supplement CDNs recovered the selected data; NAR supplementary methods remained inaccessible. MediaDB recipes and Biolog phenotypes alone did not establish quantitative growth targets.

## Use and selection rationale

**Shared mapping and constraints.** Map nutrients through [the 185-row identity table](../../data/reference/iML1515_growth_carbon_sources.csv), verified against [iML1515.json](../../models/iML1515.json); its biomass column is simulated. Oxaloacetate has no exchange; retained subsets need vocabulary expansion, not GEM changes. Preserve basal phosphate, ammonium, sulfate, metals and counterions; fixed unmapped trace borate remains metadata. Concentrations are features, not uptake caps; missing measurements do not close exchanges. Oxygen uptake is **unmeasured in all five**: record aeration and explicit oxygen assumptions. Strain regulation and kinetics remain limitations; predictive accuracy is untested.

**Okano2020.** Normalize D-prefixed labels, merge repeated singles across sheets, and exclude engineered-strain sheets. Key mappings include `EX_g6p_e`, `EX_lcts_e`, `EX_melib_e`, `EX_fuc__L_e`, `EX_sbt__D_e`; cultures were shaken at 250 rpm, 37°C. Replicated quantitative mixture coverage gives the strongest overall balance; retain exponential targets, separating diauxic phases from unique media.

**Gerosa2015.** Parse mean/SD strings—glucose is 0.65 ± 0.01—and use composition plus eight μ targets. Mapping follows the identity table, including acetate `EX_ac_e`; 35 mL cultures occupied 500 mL flasks at 300 rpm, 37°C. Strong measurement provenance supports its rank; exclude uptake, secretion and yield inputs because methods multiply concentration-versus-biomass slopes by the growth target.

**Wang2019.** Normalize `Pyruvare` to pyruvate, remove mirrored pairs/placeholders, and retain three experimental blocks before averaging. Map sources through the identity table; growth measurements used microplates at 900 rpm, 37°C. Reserving galactose/malate leaves 25 of 38 positive recipes, providing broader replicated coverage than Hermsen; no-growth pairs are a separate phenotype.

**Hermsen2015.** Use column E, `measured growth rate on both substrates`, with substrate names A/B; exclude single-source growth labels C/D and theoretical predictions F/G from inputs. Map the seven sources through the identity table and record fixed IPTG/aerated batch culture; supplementary methods calculate uptake using μ, so exclude those fluxes. It adds independently measured mixtures, but shared media with Okano must be grouped appropriately when pooling studies; inherited You2013 single-source measurements are not new independent experiments.

**Bajpe2026.** Filter `project=SNPv1`, batch/WT/unevolved, numeric μ and conditions ending `_NH4Cl`, then collapse identical replicate summaries: 37 measured carbon recipes minus methylpyruvate and β-methyl-D-galactoside, which lack exchanges, gives 35. Map mucic acid to `EX_galct__D_e`, dulcitol to `EX_galt_e`, and explicitly split DL-malate between `EX_mal__D_e`/`EX_mal__L_e`; metadata records O₂, not its flux. Its diversity complements mixtures, but reserve the 16 alternative-nitrogen recipes until basal-ammonium replacement is clarified, and do not count legacy PRECISE rows or the overlapping PRJNA1273304 atlas again.
